## 4. The story formula (studied from 13 top videos; 7 transcripts read)
Full detail and checklist: `production/in-this-town/hit-formula.md`. The 10 beats:
1. **The rule in one sentence:** "In this town, …"
2. **"You see,"** + how it works + the cost.
3. **3–5 clever ways the town adapts**, e.g. "the mailman delivers on a pogo stick because a bounce only counts as one".
4. **"My name is…":** the hero is different, or mocked.
5. **The PLANT:** a skill, object or saying that matters later.
6. **Someone they love in danger** by the halfway point.
7. **A countdown in numbers:** "300… 250… 3, 2, 1, zero." / "11:55. 11:59. 12:00."
8. **The twist:** the rule's exact wording + the plant ("it only counts steps your FEET take", so he walks on his hands).
9. **The town changes.**
10. **A button line under 8 words:** "Everyone except Theo." / "Recess." / "Your tie is red."

**The numbers:**
- **Length and pace:** the biggest hits run **2:00–2:55**, at **about 3 words a second**, with one first-person narrator.
- **Sound words** are written in ("Knock. Knock. Knock.", "Snip!").
- **Titles:** 3–7 words + 1 emoji ("Everyone Has A String On Their Back 🧶").

**Comment engines:**
- **An arguable loophole:** "why not use cars?" got 18K likes.
- **A named side character's funny moment:** "Except Theo 😭" got 20K likes.
- **Rules with numbers** that people do maths on.

**Look:**
- Vertical 9:16; blocky avatars in lit 3D scenes.
- Close-ups on faces; rule signs as props ("BY ORDER OF THE TOWN — NO SCISSORS").
- Night scenes with coloured light, a warm ending, word-by-word captions.
- **The owner's rule:** captions and any on-screen words must be BIG ("I hate small words").

**Tone for OUR channel (teens and adults):**
- Lean into the thriller and horror side: mimics, curfews, deadly rules, tough choices, R6Rants-style dark twists.
- Also grown-up emotion: parents' sacrifices, love, loss, betrayal.
- Heroes can be teens or adults, not only small children.
- Keep it clean enough for YouTube ads: no gore, no swearing.

## 5. How the money works on YouTube (check the current rules in YouTube's help center)
- **Partner Program (ad revenue):** 1,000 subscribers **plus** either 4,000 public long-form watch hours in 12 months, or 10M Shorts views in 90 days.
  - **Shorts views don't count toward the watch hours**, so the weekly long compilations are what gets the channel into the program through watch hours.
- **Long-form ads pay far more per view than Shorts.** The long compilations are the money; Shorts are for reach.
- **"Not made for kids":**
  - Upload every video as **No, it's not made for kids.** That keeps personalised ads (higher pay) and comments.
  - YouTube also judges from the content itself: kid-targeted themes, characters and language can get a channel reclassified.
  - So keep the stories, titles and thumbnails clearly teen/adult (suspense, horror, mature emotion). Avoid nursery-style themes and "for kids" wording.
- **Never** re-upload others' clips. Use **our own characters**, not copies of Roblox's exact avatars or logo, unless the owner decides to animate inside Roblox Studio.
