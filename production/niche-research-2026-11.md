# Niche research: evergreen, search-driven faceless channel (Nov 8, 2026)

**Goal:** a channel that earns from **search** (people with a problem typing it into YouTube), not from lucky recommendations. It should be faceless, made with AI voice and visuals, and long-lasting ("evergreen").

**Data sources:** FlowMap niche reports (top 20 videos of the last 12 months per niche), vidIQ keyword research (monthly searches and competition, US), vidIQ outlier search (small channels beating their averages), and channel stats of small faceless competitors.

**Why it's evergreen:** each video earns a small, steady trickle forever, about 20–100 views a day, because every day new drivers see a light come on and search it. **The supply is almost endless:**
- 5,000+ OBD fault codes (P, U, B and C codes)
- every warning light × every car brand (Toyota, Ford, Honda, Hyundai…)
- "symptoms of a bad ___" for every car part

**Ad value:** auto is one of the better ad categories, because the viewers are US drivers about to spend money on repairs or parts. vidIQ's calculator estimates about **$170–460 per 100K US long-form views**, a conservative estimate. It also leaves room for **affiliate links** (OBD2 scanners, parts) once the channel is monetized.

## 3. Recommendation

**Start a faceless car channel: warning lights, fault codes and "symptoms of a bad ___"** in English for a US audience. It beats the other options because:
1. **People search it with urgency** ("my check engine light is flashing"), so it doesn't depend on luck.
2. **Competition is low** (24–38), and small faceless channels are proven to grow there.
3. **The audience is US-heavy**, so ad value is high, with affiliate income on top.
4. **It fits your production:** AI voice, plus our 3D engine-part animations and clear diagrams. The whole video is an explanation, not a repair filmed on camera.
5. **The topic supply is unlimited** and never goes out of date.

**Runner-up:** the same model for **home appliance error codes**, which could be a second channel later.

**Blonxin:** add TV and streaming "fix it" videos (Samsung TV black screen, Fire TV remote not working…). That's search traffic on the channel you already own.

## 4. Risks (honest)
- **YouTube's "inauthentic / mass-produced content" rule (July 2025):** template-spam AI channels get demonetized. Each video must add real value: correct causes, safe step-by-step checks, and visuals made for that video. No word-swapped copies.
- **Accuracy matters:** wrong advice about brakes can hurt people. Every script needs a fact check. Add a "when to see a mechanic" line, and never recommend unsafe or illegal bypasses (for example, catalytic converter or O2 sensor "delete" tricks, which break emissions law in the US).
- **It's slow at first:** expect hundreds of views per video in the first months. The income comes from building a library of 100–200 videos (Car Fix School reached 1.76M views in 17 months).
