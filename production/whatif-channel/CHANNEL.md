# Milo's Odds: channel setup

## Name
**Milo's Odds**. The handle is **@milosodds**. If that handle is taken, use @milosoddsTV or @themilosodds.

Why this name: it joins the character (Milo) and the signature device (the Survival Odds meter) into a short, unique brand. Plain "What If", "Survival Odds", "Milo What If" and "Worst Case Milo" already exist as channels (several were created in October 2026), so they would be confused with copycats.

## Icon (800×800 PNG; YouTube crops it to a circle)
- `icon-A-milo-odds.png` (recommended): Milo's shocked face inside a red odds ring at 75%. A face gets more clicks in a feed than a symbol.
- `icon-B-emblem.png`: the empty dashed Sun with the red odds ring. Use it as the video watermark or end-screen mark.

## Description (paste as-is)
What if the Sun disappeared, and you had 8 minutes left?

Every episode, Milo gets dropped into one impossible "what if": the Sun vanishing, falling into a black hole, the oceans boiling, a world with no Moon. We follow him minute by minute, using real science, and keep score with his Survival Odds.

Can he make it? Could you?

Animated science stories. Real physics, sourced facts, no filler.
New episode every week.

Tell us in the comments what Milo should survive next, and guess his final odds before the end.
