# Drive or Tow: the first 10 videos

All search numbers are vidIQ, US, monthly, checked Nov 2026. Competition runs from 0 to 100, and lower is easier. This list replaces the draft list in `production/niche-research-2026-11.md`.

## How the 10 were picked
1. **Demand:** at least 3,000 US searches a month for the main phrase.
2. **Low competition:** 22–41 on the main phrase. Phrases over 45 were dropped ("car won't start" at 55, "alternator" at 48).
3. **A real verdict:** each topic gets a clear answer of DRIVE, CAREFUL or TOW in the first 15 seconds. That answer is the channel's signature.
4. **A real "Bill, cheapest first":** each topic has free or cheap checks to try before the expensive repair. That saves the viewer money, which is what earns trust and comments.
5. **Videos feed each other:** the code videos link to the warning-light videos, and video 10 is the hub that links to all of them.

## Upload order (why this order)
- **Starts with video 01:** the script is already written.
- **Mixes the verdict colours:** a TOW video (02) comes early, so the channel isn't "amber every time".
- **Ends with the hub (10):** "Every dashboard light" is the biggest search (13K). It goes last so every light in it can link to a full video that already exists, which keeps viewers on the channel.

## 01. Check Engine Light Flashing? Do This Before You Drive Another Mile
- **Status:** script ready (`scripts/drive-or-tow-01-check-engine-light-flashing.md`). Waiting for the voice recording.
- **Hook:** "Your check engine light is flashing. Here's the answer first."
- **Angle:** solid means "soon" and flashing means "now". Unburned fuel cooks the catalytic converter.
- **Bill:** read the code → ignition coil (free swap test) → spark plugs → catalytic converter.
- **Thumbnail:** a giant flashing amber engine lamp, with a red-hot converter in the x-ray car below. Text: "DRIVE OR TOW?"

## 02. Oil Pressure Light On? Turn the Engine Off. Here's Why
- **Verdict:** TOW, the red one. The channel shows early that it will tell you to stop.
- **Hook:** "Of every light on your dashboard, this is the one where seconds count."
- **Angle: the dipstick decides.**
  - Many cars have two oil warnings: low oil *level* (often amber) and low oil *pressure* (the red oil can).
  - The red one means the engine isn't getting oil. Pull over safely and switch it off.
  - Then check the dipstick:
    - Oil is low: top up, and the light may go out.
    - Oil is full and the light stays on: tow it.
- **Bill:** dipstick check (free) → top up oil → oil pressure sensor (the most common cheap culprit) → oil pump → engine.
- **X-ray:** oil flowing through the engine, then drying up while the bearings glow red.
- **Thumbnail:** a red oil-can lamp on a black cluster. Text: "ENGINE OFF. NOW."

## 03. Battery Light On While Driving? Don't Turn the Engine Off
- **Verdict:** CAREFUL. It becomes TOW if the temperature gauge climbs.
- **Hook:** "That's not really a battery light. It's a charging light."
- **Angle:**
  - The alternator has stopped charging, so the car is running on what's left in the battery. Switch off everything you don't need and drive straight to a shop or home.
  - **Don't switch the engine off** on the way, because it may not restart.
  - **Watch the temperature gauge:** on many cars the same belt also drives the water pump.
- **Bill:** look at the belt and the battery terminals (free) → free charging-system test at a parts store → battery → alternator.
- **X-ray:** the belt snaps and the alternator goes dark. Then a battery meter drains.
- **Thumbnail:** the battery lamp, with a key and a big red ✕. Text: "DON'T TURN IT OFF".

## 04. P0420 Code: Is Your Catalytic Converter Really Dead? Check These First
- **Verdict:** DRIVE. It's usually safe for now, but it will fail an emissions test.
- **Hook:** "P0420 doesn't mean buy a converter. It means check four things first."
- **Angle:**
  - The code means the rear oxygen sensor thinks the converter isn't cleaning well enough. Sometimes the sensor is wrong, or air leaks in ahead of it.
  - Links back to video 01: misfires are what kill converters.
  - **We never show O2 "spacer" or delete tricks.** They are illegal under US emissions law.
- **Bill:** read the other codes and the freeze-frame data (free) → exhaust leak → rear O2 sensor → catalytic converter.
- **X-ray:** two O2 sensors, one before the converter and one after. Their wave graphs are shown side by side: the sensor after a healthy converter reads flat, and after a dying one it moves like the first sensor.
- **Thumbnail:** "P0420" in huge type, with the converter glowing in the x-ray. Text: "DON'T BUY IT YET".

## 05. P0171 System Too Lean: The Cheap Fix to Try First
- **Verdict:** DRIVE, and get it fixed soon.
- **Hook:** "Your engine is getting too much air, or not enough fuel. Here's how to tell which, cheaply."
- **Angle:**
  - An x-ray balance scale, with air on one side and fuel on the other.
  - Top causes: a vacuum leak, a dirty mass airflow sensor, or weak fuel pressure.
  - The MAF cleaning step also captures the "maf sensor cleaning" and "mass airflow sensor" searches.
- **Bill:** listen for a hiss and check the hoses (free) → clean the MAF sensor (a can of MAF cleaner) → cracked intake or PCV hose → fuel filter or fuel pump.
- **Thumbnail:** "P0171" with a tipped balance scale, AIR heavy and FUEL light. Text: "TRY THIS FIRST".

## 06. Tire Pressure Light Won't Go Off? Free Fixes First
- **Verdict:** DRIVE, once the tires are at the pressure on the door sticker. It's CAREFUL if one tire is clearly low.
- **Hook:** "If this light comes on every cold morning, your tires aren't broken. Physics is."
- **Angle:**
  - Cold air drops tire pressure, by about 1 psi for every 10°F.
  - A light that is **solid** means a tire is low.
  - A light that **flashes for about a minute and then stays on** means a sensor or system fault.
  - Many cars need a reset or relearn after you add air.
- **Bill:** air to the door-sticker pressure (free to $2) → reset or relearn → a sensor (often a dead sensor battery).
- **Thumbnail:** the TPMS horseshoe lamp with a snowflake. Text: "IT'S NOT YOUR TIRE".

## 07. Car Shaking? When It Shakes Tells You Why
- **Verdict:** four verdicts, one for each situation. It's a natural chapter video.
- **Hook:** "Tell me *when* it shakes, and I'll tell you *what* it is."
- **Angle, "the Shake Finder":**

  | When it shakes | Likely cause | Verdict |
  |---|---|---|
  | At highway speed | Tire balance | DRIVE |
  | When braking | Warped rotors | CAREFUL |
  | At idle | A misfire or an engine mount | CAREFUL |
  | Shaking plus a flashing check engine light | See video 01 | TOW |

- **Bill:** look at the tires (free) → wheel balance → brake rotors → engine mounts.
- **Thumbnail:** an x-ray car with 4 numbered shake zones. Text: "WHEN = WHY".

## 08. Car Won't Start: Battery or Starter? Listen to the Sound
- **Verdict:** DRIVE once it starts after a jump. TOW if it won't crank at all.
- **Hook:** "Don't guess. Turn the key, and listen."
- **Angle, "the Sound Test":** real sound effects are the star here, and they stay at low volume.

  | What you hear | What it usually means |
  |---|---|
  | Fast clicking | Weak battery |
  | One click | Starter or a bad connection |
  | Cranks but won't fire | Fuel or spark |
  | Nothing, and the dash is dark | Dead battery or loose terminals |

- **Bill:** clean and tighten the terminals (free) → jump start (free) → free battery test at a parts store → new battery → starter.
- **Thumbnail:** a key, with the sound wave as a graphic. Text: "CLICK CLICK = ?".

## 09. How to Reset the Check Engine Light (And When You Shouldn't)
- **Verdict:** DRIVE, but resetting isn't fixing.
- **Hook:** "You can turn that light off in thirty seconds. Here's why that can cost you."
- **Angle:**
  - There are three ways to clear the light:
    1. Fix the cause. The light usually clears itself after some driving.
    2. Clear it with a scan tool.
    3. Disconnect the battery. This also wipes radio and idle settings.
  - **The trap:** clearing the code also resets the car's readiness monitors, so an emissions test right after will be refused as "not ready". It also erases the clue a mechanic needs, so write the code down first.
- **Bill:** read and write down the code (free) → scan tool → fix the cause.
- **Thumbnail:** an engine lamp with a "RESET" button and a warning sign. Text: "DON'T JUST RESET IT".

## 10. Every Dashboard Warning Light Explained: Drive, Careful or Tow
- **Verdict:** all three. The whole video is the traffic-light system.
- **Hook:** "Every light on your dashboard falls into one of three groups."
  - Red: stop.
  - Amber: deal with it soon.
  - Green and blue: just information.
- **Angle:**
  - The hub video, sorted by verdict instead of A–Z.
  - Each light gets about 20 seconds, then "full video here" (cards and the end screen point to videos 01–09).
  - At about 10–12 minutes, it's the channel's longest video, which is good for watch time.
- **Thumbnail:** a full cluster split into three columns (green, amber and red). Text: "DRIVE · CAREFUL · TOW".
