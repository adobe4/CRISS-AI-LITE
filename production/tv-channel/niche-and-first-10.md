## Five content pillars (one channel, many angles)
1. **Hidden settings and features:** the channel's biggest winners.
2. **Free and legal TV:** FAST channels (free channels with ads, like Pluto TV), antennas, local channels.
3. **Buy smart:** which TV or box to buy, comparisons, and "lasts 10 years".
4. **Fixes:** no sound, remote not working, black screen.
5. **TV news, weekly:** price rises, new services, Roku and Google TV changes. This is what builds a fanbase (the Cord Cutters News model).

## Rules for this channel
- **100% legal:** no IPTV, no "jailbreak", no "free movies" apps. That content stays on Blonxin.
- **Real screen recordings** of TV menus for every tutorial. That keeps it clear of YouTube's mass-produced content rule and builds trust with older viewers.
- **Big text, slow and clear voice,** the same "no small words" rule. The audience is older, so this matters even more here.
- **Never invent credentials.** No claiming to be a "TV repairman" unless it's true.
- **Every price and menu name** gets checked on the date we script it.
