### The deal: how we judge it
- **Pace:** 3 videos a week, so all 30 take about 10 weeks. Every video is long (8–12 min), with no Shorts.
- **First check, at video 10 plus 30 days:** at least 3 videos should be getting steady daily views from YouTube search and suggested videos.
- **Final check, at video 30:** if fewer than 5 videos are above 1K views and nothing is growing, stop. Search channels grow slowly at first and then add up, so don't judge before video 30.
