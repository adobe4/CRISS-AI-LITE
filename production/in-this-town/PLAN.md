# In This Town: production plan (long-form only)

**How we work together:**
- **I write:** the stories, the prompts and the voice text.
- **The owner generates:** images in Nano Banana 2.1, clips in Gemini Omni Flash 1.2, and narration in MiniMax.
- **I edit:** the video in Remotion (motion on stills, big captions, SFX, chapters, thumbnail).

## Episode format
- 16:9, 18–25 min, made for TV.
- 20-second cold open, then 6 stories of 2–3 min.
- Between stories: a "rulebook" card. At the end: a teaser of the next rule.
- Chapters: one per rule.

## Pipeline per story
1. **Script:** 10-beat formula, about 300 words, voice text with numbers written as words.
2. **Prompts:** 4 reference images (once per cast), about 24 shots, 3 clips (hook, twist, payoff).
3. **The owner generates and uploads:** `sNN_XX.png`, `sNN_cX.mp4`, `sNN_voice.mp3`.
4. **I build:**
   - whisper word timings;
   - every still gets motion (push, parallax, shake, fog, snow);
   - clips at the 3 key beats;
   - big captions, SFX at 0.025, no music.
5. **Review:** stills or a draft goes to the owner, then the final render.
