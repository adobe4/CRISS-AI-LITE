# Story bible: Harrowmere (the town)

**The town:** a small foggy blocky town with tall narrow houses, a clock tower in a small square, old street lamps, and pine woods and hills behind. It's peaceful by day and slightly eerie at night. Every story is a different rule of Harrowmere.

**Style (put at the start of EVERY image prompt):**
> Stylized 3D render in a blocky toy-figure style: characters have square block heads with simple painted faces (dot eyes, small expressive mouth, bold eyebrows), rectangular torsos, blocky arms and legs, smooth matte plastic material, like a polished low-poly game animation. Cinematic lighting, soft shadows, gentle depth of field, rich but natural colour. Widescreen 16:9 frame. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark.

**HANDS (put in EVERY prompt with a character):**
> HANDS: small mitten-style block hands — one rounded block for the palm and fingers together, with only shallow grooves between the fingers, plus a separate small thumb. No separate long fingers, no claws, no C-shaped hands.

**Rules for images:**
- Faces are always simple painted DOT eyes, never big white cartoon eyes.
- Our own blocky style. No Roblox logo or UI.
- **No text in images:** signs and cards stay blank. All text is added in the edit, BIG.
- Always attach the character sheet when a character appears.
