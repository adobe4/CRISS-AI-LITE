- Background: cream (#F6F1E4), like a daftari page. Text: near-black (#151515). Accent (payback line only): red pen (#D7262E).
- Font: Roboto Black for numbers, Roboto Medium for labels (system font, no "AI font").
- Keep it on screen for at least 4 seconds. People screenshot these.

**B-roll:** your own phone photos of real stalls (genge, chipsi, kinyozi) beat stock and AI b-roll for authenticity. Ten photos taken on a single walk is enough. Use the mtaa photos for the businesses and the cards for the numbers.
