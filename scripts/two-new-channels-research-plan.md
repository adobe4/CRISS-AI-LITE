## PART 2 — YouTube rules (what kills these channels)

1. **Inauthentic content** (renamed July 2025, clarified **July 16, 2026**). Three types get you removed from monetization:
   1. **Generic, repetitive or template-based content.** This includes tutorials that just repeat what's already everywhere.
   2. **Off-putting or distressing content.**
   3. **AI personas discussing sensitive topics: health, medical, finance, legal.** ⚠️ This directly hits AI fitness channels that give health advice.
2. **AI is NOT banned.** The test is originality and value, not the tool. There is no "30% rule" or numeric threshold; those are myths.
3. **Disclosure:** tick "altered or synthetic content" when a realistic AI scene could be mistaken for real footage.

### Our protection rules (both channels)

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| No fake "expert" persona. The narrator is a **character**, never "Dr./Coach X". | Avoids category 3 |
| Fitness = **training mechanics, strength and history**. NO testosterone, supplements, steroids, injury or medical treatment, weight-loss promises. | Health is the sensitive zone |
| Every claim is sourced (study or book), with a source list in the description | Proves originality and research |
| A unique script structure per video; never reuse the same scene loops | Avoids "template" |
| An About section explaining HOW we make videos (human-written, human-reviewed, AI-assisted voice and art), like The Paint Explainer | Shows good faith |
| Max **3 long videos per week** per channel (Rep Theory's 6.5/week is a red flag) | "Mass-produced" signal |
| No Shorts until the long-form has an audience | Shorts died on Mr. Well |
| Never copy another channel's title or angle | Copies get 0.1% of the original's views anyway |

## PART 4 — CHANNEL 2: "What If" with a story twist

**Positioning:** *The big "what if" questions everyone searched, retold as a story with one character who has to survive it.*

**Why:**
- Evergreen giant demand (median views/day): black hole 4,255; last person on Earth 2,962; sun disappeared 2,369; all ice melted 2,075; everyone disappeared 2,024; Earth had rings 1,957.

### What makes us DIFFERENT
- **One named character**, an ordinary guy with a backpack, lives through every scenario. Viewers return for him.
- **Signature "Survival Odds" meter** on screen that updates as the scenario unfolds, with a final score at the end (people comment their own guess, which drives engagement).
- **The twist rule:** never "What if X?" alone. Always "What if X **and YOU** are there / have Y minutes / must do Z."
- **Science check:** each fact sourced (NASA, papers). Put a "Real or made-up?" card for the one claim viewers doubt most.
- **Length:** 7–10 min, the same as Awaken and IFOLOGY's winners.

## PART 5 — Production line (AI agent + Nano Banana)

1. **Research brief:** the top 10 existing videos on the topic, what they missed, and 5–10 sources.
2. **Script:** an original structure, a hook in 5 seconds, an open loop every ~90 seconds, the signature segment, and the next-video tease.
3. **Shot list → Nano Banana images** in the locked art style, with a character reference sheet for consistency.
4. **Voice:** one consistent narrator voice.
5. **Edit:** motion every 2–3 seconds, the on-screen meter/counter, captions.
6. **Human review:** you watch it fully before upload (this is also what the policy expects).
7. **Upload:** sources in the description, AI disclosure where realistic, chapters.

```
Watch this YouTube video completely: [LINK]

I'm studying why it performed so well. Give me:
1. HOOK: the exact first 15 seconds (word for word) and why it stops the scroll.
2. STRUCTURE: a timestamped outline of every section, with the purpose of each.
3. RETENTION DEVICES: every open loop, tease, pattern interrupt, and payoff, with timestamps.
4. SCRIPT STYLE: sentence length, tone, how claims are backed, how the viewer is addressed ("you"), words repeated.
5. VISUAL STYLE: art style, colours, character design, how often the visual changes (average seconds per shot), text on screen, zooms/motion.
6. AUDIO: voice type and pace (words per minute), music and sound effects use.
7. ENDING: how it ends and what it pushes viewers to do next.
8. WEAKNESSES: anything boring, confusing, repetitive or that looks low-effort/AI-template, with timestamps.
9. What a competitor would need to do to make a clearly BETTER and DIFFERENT video on the same topic.
```
