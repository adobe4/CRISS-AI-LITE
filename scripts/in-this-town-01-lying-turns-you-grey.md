# In This Town, short 01: "In This Town, Lying Turns You Grey"

- **Length:** about 95 seconds (about 230 words), vertical 9:16.
- **Later use:** it goes into compilation #1.

## Voice text
Paste everything between the two dashed lines. It has no labels, and numbers are written as words.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

In this town, every lie turns you a little bit grey.

A small lie gives you a grey freckle. A big lie, a grey hand.

So everyone here is bright. Red, blue, green. Nobody dares to lie.

Except my mom.

My mom is grey. All of her. Her face, her hands, even her hair.

The kids at school called her the biggest liar in town. And I believed them.

I stopped holding her hand. I walked home alone.

One night I asked her. Why are you grey? What did you lie about?

She just smiled and said, I'm fine.

And a new grey spot appeared on her cheek.

So I watched her.

At dinner, there was only one plate of food. She put it in front of me. I already ate, she said. Grey.

In winter, she gave me her coat. I'm not cold. Grey.

When the rent letter came, she hid it. Everything's okay. Grey.

Every grey spot on my mom was a lie she told so I could have more.

The next morning, I held her hand all the way to school.

And when the kids laughed, I said it out loud.

My mom is the most honest person in this town.

And I didn't turn grey at all.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
